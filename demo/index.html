How to read the results

  • Probability is calibrated: on patients similar to the NIH data, findings shown at about 10% should be present in about 1 in 10 images.
  • The raw network outputs were 2.5 to 12 times too high, so a per-class correction fitted on 8,805 validation images is applied.
  • vs typical compares the probability with how common the finding is. Well above 1× means this image looks more like the cases with that finding than an average image does.
  • The model always returns 14 numbers, even for an image that is not a chest X-ray.

Model

  • DenseNet121 (ImageNet-pretrained), trained on NIH ChestX-ray14 with the official patient-wise split: 77,719 training, 8,805 validation and 25,596 test images, 224×224 grayscale.
  • Test mean AUROC 0.803 (95% patient-level interval 0.797 to 0.808). Strongest for emphysema, hernia and cardiomegaly; weakest for infiltration, pneumonia and consolidation.
  • Chosen from three training runs by validation AUROC, not test AUROC. Runs with ONNX Runtime Web.

Known limitations

  • Images from one US hospital; labels mined from radiology report text, so some are wrong.
  • After calibration, pneumothorax is under-predicted (6.5% on average against 10.4% actual), and probabilities may be off for patients unlike the NIH population.
  • Many pneumothorax images show a chest drain, which the model may partly rely on.
  • Children, lateral views and photos of screens were not part of training.
